   - GraphQL CRUD and a REST mirror under `delivery-fees/`, using the `delivery.pricing.*` permissions.
   - Migration `1795700000000-delivery-pricing.ts`.
   - Every store gets a "Platform default prices" table, named in the store's language (lifecycle hook, plus a converge on `migrate`, D3112, D3137), seeded with indicative 2026 per-wilaya prices.

It applies the layers (commune, then wilaya, then not delivered) and answers `source: 'commune' | 'wilaya' | null`. Overweight is computed in integers. The money ceiling and malformed rows answer unresolved.
   - It is registered on the offers `SHIPPING_QUOTE_REGISTRAR`. Product weights come from a reviewed batched catalog reader (D3119).
