# Handoff — P1.S2 (Accounts & auth)

## Done
1. **`plugins/auth`.** Throttled email and password sign-in, registration (User, Business, Store and Channel in one transaction), email verification by link and OTP, forgot and reset, change password, change email, profile with photo, OAuth behind `AUTH_OAUTH_ENABLED`.
2. **`plugins/rate-limit` (added beyond the brief's owned paths, D28).** Atomic Valkey token buckets with lockout, an in-process fallback during outages, and a client-IP resolver.
3. **Dashboard pages.** `/register` (3 steps), `/verify-email`, `/confirm-email-change`, `/account/profile`, `/account/security`, the verify banner, and stock `/forgot-password` and `/reset-password`.
4. **Email.** Worker queue `auth-email`, sealed payloads, AR and EN templates in `apps/server/src/email/`.
5. **Sessions.** `sessionDuration` is 30 days; `logout` ends only the current session (ADR 0017 §3).
6. **Assets.** `AssetServerPlugin` on Garage with random names, decoded-image checks and a 40 MP cap.
7. **Evidence.** `docs/evidence/P1.S2/` from `scripts/evidence/P1.S2.sh`.

## For the user to see
- **D20 reverses a user-accepted detail of ADR 0010.** Public sign-up pages are dashboard routes. It was the only way to land on Home after sign-up without a cross-origin token handoff. Accepted under the standing P1 authorization; if you disagree, the alternative is a login step after sign-up.
- **French emails fall back to Arabic.** Templates exist in AR and EN only.
- **Facebook never auto-links to an existing account by email (D46).** Graph has no field confirming an email.
- **An account made through a provider with an unconfirmed email loses that link on its first reset (D50)**, including an honest one, which must link again.
- **The Arabic copy needs a native review.**

## Not fixed, and why
- **Real Google and Facebook were never exercised**, only the mock (PKCE and POST token exchange in particular). The inventory row is ticked with this caveat.
- **Turnstile is off by default (D35).** The challenge page is P3.S4's, so sign-up is unchallenged until then, and D27's disclosure of a taken email relies only on the IP limit. With Turnstile on before P3.S4, sign-up is refused. ADR 0008 and 0017 want it on sign-up; ADR 0012 says COD submit only.
- **`CLIENT_IP_HEADER` must be set in production** (boot fails if blank), and the origin must be reachable only through the edge, or the header is forgeable: P3.S5.
- **`Referrer-Policy`** belongs at the edge: P3.S5.
- **SMTP credentials and required STARTTLS** are not implemented, needed before production. `.env.example` keeps `SMTP_HOST=mailpit` for local use; nothing rejects it in production.
- **`i18n:check` does not exist yet**; a parity test stands in (D40) until P1.S5.
- **P1.S5 dashboard items seen in the screenshots:** the hex channel code in the switcher and the English "Administration" heading.

## Notes for later sessions
- **P1.S3:** do not grant core `UpdateAdministrator` to merchants without shadowing it: stock `update` sets password and email directly.
- **P1.S6:** `auth-email` is a named constant; whichever merges later adds it to `packages/shared/src/queues.ts` (D42).
- **Shop API:** `login` keeps the stock unthrottled native strategy. Administrator-only users cannot reach it; throttle it before customer accounts ship.
- **`verifyEmailOtp` must carry no `@Transaction`:** `attemptOtp` commits on its own connection.
- **`permittedFileTypes` is PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP** until the catalog widens it; `PresetOnlyStrategy` is set.
- **Rotating `AUTH_TOKEN_SECRET`** voids queued mail, tokens and OAuth state.
- The first stock `/profile` page stays but is restricted on the server (D38).
