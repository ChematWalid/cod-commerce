> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. Skills: `tdd-workflow` (every task), `database-migrations` (Task 10), `security-review` (Task 16), `verification-loop` + `superpowers:verification-before-completion` (before every PASS claim).

**Goal:** A pure, exhaustively tested pricing engine and `PricingService` (Nest provider, no HTTP) that turns a cart into a quote (lines, discounts, applied/skipped with reasons, rejected codes, nudge, suggestions, delivery block, totals, signed `qt1` token) for the quantity, free-delivery, product-discount and order-discount kinds plus discount codes, with offer/code tables scoped by business and store.

**Architecture:** New feature plugin `plugins/offers` (copy of `plugins/_template`, wiring modelled on `plugins/catalog`). The engine is pure TypeScript in `plugins/offers/domain/` (no Nest/TypeORM/Vendure): a pipeline over an immutable `PricingSnapshot` (offers, codes, resolved unit prices, collection membership, usage counts, shipping quote) with one adapter per offer kind in a registry (§3a.7). `application/PricingService` loads the snapshot through ports (offer repository, `UnitPriceResolver` chain, collection membership, shipping quote, buyer history, usage counts — each a registrar seam like catalog's `SOLD_OUT_SOURCE_REGISTRAR`), runs the engine and signs the token. Shared request/response types, zod schemas, reasons and money helpers live in `packages/shared/src/pricing/`.

**Tech Stack:** Vendure 3.8.1, NestJS 12.4.0, TypeORM 0.3.31 (Postgres 17), zod 4.6.7, Vitest 5.1.0 (+ new `@vitest/coverage-v8` 5.1.0), `node:crypto` (HMAC-SHA256, SHA-256).

## Review Focus

1. **Duplicate or messy cart input** (Task 8): two request lines of the same variant (`{v1,1}`, `{v1,1}`) price as one line `key=v1, quantity=2` and the 2-piece tier applies; the token `h` equals the hash of the merged cart. Test: `pipeline.test.ts` "merges two lines of one variant before tier counting".
2. **Codes typed loosely** (Task 8): `" influ10 "` applies the `INFLU10` offer; the same code twice gives one application and `rejectedCodes: [{code:'INFLU10', reason:'duplicate'}]`; a code of business B or of store A2 gives `not_found`. Tests: `codes.test.ts` "normalises case and spaces", "rejects a repeated code as duplicate", `offers-isolation.int.test.ts` "rejects a code of another business as not found".
3. **Discounts larger than prices** (Tasks 6-7): a fixed product discount of 6,000 on a 4,900 unit gives unit price 0, never negative; an order discount of 20,000 on a 9,800 subtotal gives `discountTotal` 9,800; `total` never goes below the delivery price. Tests: `product-discount.test.ts` "clamps a fixed discount at the unit price", `order-discount.test.ts` "never discounts more than the subtotal".
4. **Foreign or unknown variant in the cart** (Tasks 11-12): a variant of store A2 or of business B in a quote for store A1 is not priced from B's data: the quote has `available:false`, `quoteToken:null`, `unavailableVariantIds:[that id]`. Test: `offers-isolation.int.test.ts` "does not price a variant of another store".
5. **Token at the edge of its life and after rotation** (Task 9): a token checked at exactly `exp` fails `expired`; a token signed with kid `k1` verifies after `k2` becomes active while `k1` is still in the ring and fails `bad_signature` once `k1` is removed. Tests: `quote-token.test.ts` "expires at exp, not after", "verifies an old kid while it stays in the ring", "fails an unknown kid like a bad signature".
