# P1.S4 Team members & invitations Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Merchants invite team members by email with roles, store access, base salary and commission structures; invitees accept, become Active members on the chosen stores; owners list and manage members (limit, stop receiving orders, deactivate, remove, pay salary placeholder) and manage commission configs. Four core audit issues (#114, #160, #211, #139) are fixed first, test first.

**Architecture:** New feature plugin `plugins/team` copied from `plugins/_template` (domain / application / infrastructure / api / dashboard / tests). Team owns `TeamMember`, `Invitation`, `CommissionConfig`, `SalaryPayment` (all `TenantEntity`). Access (roles + store scope) stays in `plugins/rbac` (`MemberAccessService`); Vendure administrator creation stays in tenancy; email goes through auth's `auth-email` queue. Team reaches those through ports in `domain/ports` and adapters in `infrastructure`, over three new declared sub-entries (D206). The dashboard page fills the existing `team.members` shell leaf.

**Tech Stack:** Vendure 3.8.1 plugin, NestJS 12, TypeORM 0.3.31 (Postgres, Valkey), zod schemas in `@cod/shared`, React 19 + `@vendure/dashboard` + Lingui `.po`, Vitest (unit + integration), Playwright evidence scripts, Mailpit for mail.

**Spec:** `docs/roadmap/P1-foundation.md` section P1.S4; `docs/roadmap/handoff/P1.S{1,2,3,5}.md`; `docs/roadmap/P1-decisions.md`; `docs/audits/P1-audit.md`; `docs/research/dashboard-captures.md` ("Team") and `api-surface.md` ("Team").

## Decisions made while planning (log them as D200-D215 in Task 5, D216-D217 added by the Task 5 review; changes need the owner's say)

|---|---|---|
| D200 | Team keys members by Vendure `administratorId` (the brief's `userId?`), because rbac and the session flush key by administrator. `TeamMember.administratorId` is null while invited. | `MemberAccessService.setAccess` takes `administratorId`. |
| D201 | **Same email in two businesses:** invitations and member rows are per business and independent (unique `(businessId, emailKey)` among live rows; the same email can be invited by A and B at once). Accepting is refused with `team.account-elsewhere` when that email already has an account in another business, because a Vendure administrator carries one `customFields.businessId` and `User.identifier` is unique platform-wide. A person with two businesses needs a multi-business account model: out of scope. This **contradicts the brief's multi-tenant check** ("same email may belong to two businesses independently"), which holds only at invitation level: list it under the handoff's **Deviations** and under "For the user to see" as a **product decision for the user to review**. The invitee sees a translated (ar/en) message "this email already belongs to another business" that never names or hints at which business (no cross-tenant leak). **Approved by the coordinator 2026-11-11** under the user's standing P1 authorization. P1-decisions.md row records: *why* an account is tied to one Seller per business, which keeps tenant isolation by construction; *cost if wrong* freelance confirmation agents or agencies serving several businesses need a later migration to multi-business membership; *how "independently" is met* two businesses can each invite the same email, only the second accept is refused. | Honest reading of the brief's check within the current identity model. |
| D202 | Tenancy gains `MemberAccountService` (create a member administrator with `businessId` and no roles; soft-delete one), exported from its index. | Tenancy owns the `businessId` custom field and the superadmin context; both are restricted. |
| D203 | Auth gains `InvitedAccountService` (assert email free under the email claim lock; mark an invited address verified and set locale) and email kind `team-invitation`. | Auth owns the claim lock (D41), `emailVerifiedAt` and `emailLocale`. A mailed one-time token proves the address. |
| D204 | **rbac is the single source of truth for access (roles + stores) of every member who holds an administrator.** `TeamMember` stores no `roleIds`/`storeIds`: an invited member's intended grant lives on its `Invitation` (`grant: { roleIds, storeAccess, storeIds }`); a deactivated member's pre-deactivation grant lives in `TeamMember.suspendedAccess` (jsonb). Deactivate = snapshot into `suspendedAccess`, `status = inactive`, rbac `setAccess` with `roleIds: []`, sessions flushed. Reactivate = restore from `suspendedAccess` through `setAccess`, clear it. rbac refuses `setAccess` (including the GraphQL `setMemberAccess` mutation) on a suspended member through a `MemberSuspensionCheck` extension point declared by rbac's member-access sub-entry (default: nobody suspended; team registers the implementation), so re-granting roles cannot silently undo a deactivation. Remove = clear access, soft-delete the administrator, set `removedAt`. The business owner can do neither. A deactivated or removed account keeps/loses its login as follows: deactivated = can still sign in with stock `Authenticated` and the auth self-service (change email/password) but holds no Business Role and sees nothing (accepted; no login block, noted in the handoff); removed = administrator soft-deleted, sessions deleted. | Reuses D60 escalation rules; no duplicate state to diverge. |
| D205 | Invitation token: 32 random bytes, stored as SHA-256 hex only; link `${dashboardUrl}/accept-invitation?token=`. TTL 7 days, resend cooldown 60 s, `resendCount` max `INVITATION_MAX_RESENDS = 5`, resend replaces the hash (previous token dead). Abuse caps: pending invitations per business `MAX_PENDING_INVITATIONS = 50`; rate-limit policies `team-invite-business`, `team-invite-actor`, `team-resend-actor` (in `plugins/rate-limit/domain/policies.ts`, unit-tested) plus `invitation-accept-ip` on the public routes. Token lifecycle hygiene: the accept page strips `token` from the URL right after reading it (`history.replaceState`); preview and accept responses send `Cache-Control: no-store` and `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`; request/access logs never record the query string of `/accept-invitation` and `/team/invitations/preview` (log the path only). The public surface is **REST only** (no public GraphQL `acceptInvitation`). | 256-bit tokens need no HMAC key; no secret plumbing; the platform must not be a mail relay. |
| D206 | Three new declared sub-entries, added to `PLUGIN_SUB_ENTRIES`, each package's `exports`, and the `no-cross-plugin-internals` `pathNot`: `@cod/plugin-rbac/member-access` (`RbacPlugin`, `MemberAccessService`, `GrantPreviewService`, `MemberSuspensionCheck`, activity types), `@cod/plugin-auth/invitations` (`AuthPlugin`, `AuthEmailService`, `InvitedAccountService`), and tenancy's index (already allowed) for `MemberAccountService`. Importers allowed by the new `*-entry` arch rules: `plugins/*/infrastructure/**` **and** plugin root `*.plugin.ts` files (Nest module wiring), plus the owning plugin itself (`^plugins/rbac/`, `^plugins/auth/`); `application/`, `domain/` and `api/` never import them. | The architecture forbids other cross-plugin imports; Nest DI needs the module class. |
| D207 | Pay salary needs `team.members.edit`; the Salary column and salary fields in views are returned only to holders of `team.members.edit` (others get `null`). `requireFeature(FEATURE_KEYS.TeamSeats, scope)` runs on invite and accept (no-op until P9.S1; the brief's `'team'` key does not exist). Commission structures on a member are `commissionConfigIds`. | Catalogue has no salary key; P6.S4 adds the finance gate with the ledger. |
| D208 | The accept route resolves `Invitation` by token hash with one allow-listed unscoped read (`infrastructure/invitation-token-lookup.ts`, added to `packages/config/eslint/raw-data-access.mjs` **and** the mirror `RAW_DATA_ALLOWED_FILES` in `packages/config/tests/eslint-raw-data.test.ts`; ADR 0002: RLS is not built yet, so the unscoped read works, and P10.S2 RLS must keep this one lookup as an explicit exception); everything after runs under the invitation's own `TenantScope`. At accept, rbac `setAccess` runs with the **inviter** as actor, so an inviter who lost privilege or was removed makes the invitation fail. Invite time runs `GrantPreviewService.previewGrant` (new, Task 6) so a refusal shows immediately. | A token only ever reaches its own business; privilege is re-checked when it takes effect. |
| D209 | `GET /employees/order-managers` returns active members with `receivingOrders = true` (id, name, `maxOpenOrders`, store access); P4.S4 narrows it by order permissions. | Brief gives only the path. |
| D211 | **Store-reach rule for team actions.** An actor without all-store access may list, view and act on (limit, receiving, salary, pay, commissions, update, deactivate, remove, resend, revoke) only members whose stores lie within the actor's reach; `invite` and `update` apply the same rule to the target grant. Out-of-reach members are filtered from lists and answer `team.member-not-found` (404) on direct access. Pure rule in `domain/member-reach.ts`; actor reach comes from `MemberAccessPort.reachOf`. | `requirePermission` checks the permission on the active store only; team endpoints are business-level. |
| D212 | **Salary safety.** `pay` takes a client `idempotencyKey` (1..64 chars): unique `(businessId, memberId, idempotencyKey)`, a repeat returns the first payment and publishes nothing; the amount is read from the member row under a row lock (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`) in the same transaction as the insert. An actor may not pay, or edit the salary/commissions of, their own member row (`team.self-action`, 403); salary and commission edits need `team.members.edit`. | Two clicks must not book two payments; P6.S4 books `SalaryPaid` once. |
| D213 | **Accept ordering and locking.** A team `BusinessLockPort` (transaction-scoped `pg_advisory_xact_lock` keyed by business) is always taken **before** any rbac lock; every team service that calls rbac `setAccess` takes it first (invite, accept, update, lifecycle, remove, resend, revoke). Accept order inside one transaction: (1) claim the token by the conditional `UPDATE ... SET acceptedAt WHERE tokenHash = ? AND acceptedAt IS NULL AND revokedAt IS NULL AND expiresAt > now`, zero rows = `invitation-invalid`; (2) `assertEmailFree`; (3) create account; (4) mark verified (only after 1-3 succeeded); (5) `setAccess` as inviter; (6) member -> active. Every `RbacError`, `MemberNotFoundError`, `RoleNotFoundError`, archived-store error and a unique violation on the email at accept map to one opaque `team.invitation-invalid` (or `account-elsewhere` for the email case), roll the transaction back (token stays open) and, when permanently unusable (inviter gone), mark the invitation `revokedAt` in a follow-up transaction. Public preview answers one generic response for unknown, revoked and expired tokens; the distinct `invitation-expired` code appears only on accept after the hash matched. | No two accounts, no 500 on a race, no token oracle. |
| D214 | Roles named in a pending invitation or a deactivated member's `suspendedAccess` are not tracked by rbac's "in use" check; `delete-role` may remove them. Accept then fails closed (D213); reactivate fails with `team.access-unavailable` (409) leaving the member inactive. Recorded in the handoff for P1.S6/audit follow-up. | Keeps team out of rbac's role rules. |
| D215 | **Owner and every administrator appear in Team.** The converge creates a `TeamMember` row for **every** live business administrator that holds an rbac role assignment and has none (the owner included, idempotent); the demo seed (`apps/server/src/seed/seed-demo-members.ts`) and any member created through rbac `MemberAccessService` outside Team are picked up by the same converge. | Otherwise they would be missing from `/team` and unmanageable. |
| D216 | **Team activity diff shape.** Every `team.member.*`, `team.invitation.*` and `team.salary.paid` row's diff is exactly `{ memberId, displayName (name or null, never an email), changedFields (field names only) }`; `team.commission.*` rows `{ commissionConfigId, name, changedFields }`. Strict zod schemas `teamMemberActivityDiffSchema` / `teamCommissionActivityDiffSchema` in `@cod/shared` (Task 5 review fix); the team activity adapter parses each diff through them before writing. Never a salary, paid amount, commission value, email, token or hash. | The log needs only `settings.activity-log.view` and renders the raw diff; salaries are for `team.members.edit` holders (D207). |
| D217 | **Team activity rows are all-store only.** Team rows carry no `storeId`; rbac's activity query (Task 6) shows rows whose action is in `ALL_STORES_ONLY_ACTIVITY_ACTIONS` (every `team.*` action, `@cod/shared`) only to all-store viewers. | rbac shows storeless rows to everyone; otherwise an S1-only viewer reads events about S2-only and all-store members that D211 hides. |

## Review Focus

Inputs and conditions the brief implies but no acceptance line pins; each has a test in the named task.

1. **Double click / concurrent resend and accept** (Task 11): resend sends one mail inside the cooldown; two real parallel accepts of one token against Postgres leave exactly one administrator and one success, the other gets `team.invitation-invalid`, never a 500 (D213).
2. **Invitee whose address already has an account elsewhere**, or in this business as a non-member (Task 11: `team.account-elsewhere`, no account created, token stays open, `markInvitedVerified` never ran).
3. **Inviter loses privilege or is removed before accept** (Task 11: refused with the opaque code, no administrator left behind, rollback; permanently unusable invitation gets `revokedAt`).
4. **Email case and whitespace** (`Foo@X.com ` equals `foo@x.com` for the duplicate check and against the Vendure administrator address, in A only, not across businesses) (Task 5 schema tests, Task 11).
5. **Roles or stores from another business in the invite body**, an archived store, an empty roles list, a non-integer or negative salary (Task 5 schema tests, Task 11 integration: every one refused, nothing stored, no mail).
6. **Privilege escalation through Team** (Task 11 and 13): a Manager-level actor inviting with the Owner role or all stores gets 403 with nothing stored and no mail; an S1-only actor inviting onto S2 is refused; `PATCH /employees/:id` by a non-owner cannot lift a member above the actor's reach nor edit an Owner holder; after accept the administrator holds only stock `Authenticated` plus the chosen Business Role bindings (never `UpdateAdministrator`).
7. **Store-reach on non-access actions** (Task 12 and 13, D211): an S1-only actor cannot list, view, limit, set receiving, pay, edit salary/commissions of a member who lives on S2.
8. **Deactivation cannot be undone sideways** (Task 13, D204): rbac `setMemberAccess` on a deactivated member is refused; reactivate after a role edit restores the snapshot or fails closed.
9. **Pay salary exactly once** (Task 13, D212): parallel pays with one idempotency key produce one row and one event; self-pay refused.
10. **Secrets never leak** (Task 11, Task 17): raw token and hash absent from every GraphQL/REST response, activity-log rows and server logs; evidence contains no token or password.
