# Tech stack

Status: proposed, 2026-11-07.

**Version policy (applies everywhere)**:
- Exact pins in `package.json` (no `^`/`~`). Lockfile committed. Renovate proposes, humans merge.
- `@vendure/*` versions move only through M.S1 (ADR 0001).
- Base images are pinned by digest. Managed services are pinned by major version.
- "Latest patch" means patches are taken within a week after green CI and staging.

Citation keys: [VN] vendure-v3.md, [EH] edge-hosting-costs.md, [BK] background knowledge to verify.
